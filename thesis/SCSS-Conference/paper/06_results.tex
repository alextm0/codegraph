\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

We evaluate CodeGraph on the 300 instances of SWE-bench Lite. First, we present the quantitative comparison against the baselines. Second, we analyze the seed component ablation and runtime characteristics. Finally, we discuss the reachability bottleneck and review concrete retrieval cases.

\subsection{Quantitative Performance}

The final CodeGraph configuration achieves 74.0\% Recall@10, representing a 24.3 percentage point absolute improvement over the basic BM25 baseline. Table~\ref{tab:main-results} presents the performance trajectory and comparison.

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \caption{Main quantitative comparison on SWE-bench Lite (300 instances).}
    \label{tab:main-results}
    \begin{adjustbox}{max width=\linewidth}
    \begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Configuration} & \textbf{R@5} & \textbf{R@10} & \textbf{MRR} & \textbf{Zero-Recall} \\
        \midrule
        BM25-only (Basic) & 37.7\% & 49.7\% & 0.248 & 151 \\
        One-hop Ego Graph & 37.7\% & 41.3\% & 0.233 & 176 \\
        CodeGraph (Initial Baseline) & 37.0\% & 42.0\% & 0.228 & 174 \\
        \midrule
        CodeGraph (Uniform PPR restart) & 55.3\% & 60.7\% & 0.344 & 118 \\
        BM25-only (Seed-Engineered) & 46.7\% & 58.0\% & 0.354 & 126 \\
        \textbf{CodeGraph (Final Optimal)} & \textbf{60.7\%} & \textbf{74.0\%} & \textbf{0.442} & \textbf{78} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{adjustbox}
\end{table}

The improvement from the initial baseline (42.0\% R@10) to the optimal configuration was driven by two key revisions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Uniform PPR Restart (+18.7pp):} Switching from confidence-weighted restart to a uniform probability distribution over extracted seeds prevented ambiguous, high-confidence lexical matches from misdirecting the PageRank random walk. Uniform restart lets graph topology arbitrate relevance by concentrating probability mass where structural pathways converge.
    \item \textbf{Seed-First Engineering (+11.6pp):} Implementing compound tokenization (decomposing snake\_case and CamelCase identifiers), expanding the BM25 indexing corpus to include file paths, and applying IDF weighting to seeds improved reachability by ensuring task descriptions resolved to the correct structural entry points.
\end{enumerate}

To check the statistical significance of these results, we perform bootstrap resampling with 1,000 trials over the 300 instances. The 95\% confidence intervals for Recall@10 are [69.2\%, 78.8\%] for the final CodeGraph configuration, and [44.1\%, 55.3\%] for the basic BM25 baseline. A paired t-test confirms that CodeGraph's improvement is statistically significant (p < 0.001).

\subsection{The Reachability Bottleneck}

A defining characteristic of CodeGraph is its bimodal rank distribution: target files are either retrieved in the top-5 or not retrieved in the top-10 at all. As Figure~\ref{fig:bimodal} shows, only 5\% of instances fall into the intermediate band (ranks 6--10).

\begin{figure*}[t]
    \centering
    \begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{assets/bimodal_rank_distribution.png}
        \caption{Bimodal rank distribution for the initial configuration. Ground-truth files land almost exclusively in ranks 1--5 or outside the top-10, indicating the reachability ceiling.}
        \label{fig:bimodal}
    \end{minipage}\hfill
    \begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{assets/failure_taxonomy.png}
        \caption{Failure taxonomy of the 78 zero-recall instances, categorizing failures into Connectivity Gaps (40 cases) and Semantic Gaps (38 cases).}
        \label{fig:taxonomy}
    \end{minipage}
\end{figure*}

This bimodal pattern supports our central empirical diagnosis: the retrieval ceiling is determined by reachability, not by ranking sophistication. If the system places seeds in the correct structural neighborhood, PPR propagation ranks the target file highly. Zero-recall failures occur exclusively when the target file lies outside the reachable subgraph of the extracted seeds. This observation highlights that retrieval quality is bounded by the initial entry points, motivating our focus on seed-quality engineering.

\subsection{Qualitative Retrieval Cases}

\textbf{Success Case (Structural Amplification):} In \textit{matplotlib/matplotlib-23562}, the bug report requests support for a new 3D surface attribute and includes a traceback pointing to \texttt{art3d.py}. The lexical index identifies \texttt{Poly3DCollection} as a strong seed but also extracts noisy generic terms like \texttt{color} and \texttt{facecolors} across dozens of unrelated files. In a purely lexical baseline, the target file \texttt{lib/mpl\_toolkits/mplot3d/art3d.py} is ranked at position 31. During structural propagation, CodeGraph dissipates the probability mass of generic seeds across disconnected utility modules, while the specific \texttt{Poly3DCollection} seed propagates mass locally through direct \texttt{CALLS} and \texttt{INHERITS\_FROM} edges. The target file absorbs this structural signal and is ranked at position 1, demonstrating the graph's ability to filter lexical noise.

\textbf{Failure Case (Semantic Gap):} In \textit{django/django-10914}, the bug report requests changing the default value of the \texttt{FILE\_UPLOAD\_PERMISSIONS} setting. The issue uses conceptual terms like ``tempfile'' and ``permissions'' that match several functional modules (e.g., \texttt{storage.py}, \texttt{uploadedfile.py}), which are selected as lexical seeds. However, the target fix is in the global configuration hub, \texttt{django/conf/global\_settings.py}. Because this configuration file contains only constant definitions and lacks incoming structural dependency edges from the functional seeds, Personalized PageRank cannot propagate relevance to the target. This resulting zero-recall failure illustrates the reachability ceiling: sophisticated graph routing cannot recover a target that is logically related but structurally isolated from the initial lexical entry points.
